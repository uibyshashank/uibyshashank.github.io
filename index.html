Hospital Website Design  ·  2026

Planet Green CRM
Lead Tracking Dashboard  ·  CRM Project  ·  2026

Designed a full CRM dashboard for Planet Green's sales team to track lead status in real time. The interface surfaces the most critical information first, with role-based views for sales heads and relationship managers. I used Google Stitch for AI-assisted design and built intuitive data visualizations that give the team a clear, live picture of their pipeline without digging through spreadsheets.

QuickBooks Connector
Financial Integration UI  ·  2026

Designed the UI for a financial integration layer that syncs business activity directly into QuickBooks with zero manual entry. I covered the full flow from the login screen through to account management and transaction sync. Key design decisions included error handling for duplicate entries, clear validation states for missing fields, and a layout that makes complex financial data easy to scan for non-technical users.

Figma Financial UI Dashboard Design User Flows TechGy Innovations
Green Land Capital
Agent Lead Tracking Dashboard  ·  2026

Designed a multi-role dashboard system for Green Land Capital, a land buying and selling platform, to help officers track leads coming through multiple field agents. The challenge was structuring complex, multi-agent data into a clear, role-based interface that different types of officers could use without confusion. I designed views for screening officers, verification officers, and maintenance officers, each seeing only what they need.
